Robust Parsing of Noisy Text
A. Author, B. Author
University of Somewhere
Abstract
We study robust parsing of noisy text and show improvements on three bench-
marks with a simple augmentation strategy.
1 Introduction
Parsing noisy text is hard. Prior work focuses on clean newswire.
2 Method
We train a parser with augmented data.
3 Experiments
We evaluate on three datasets and report labelled attachment score.
4 Limitations
Our method does not handle code-switched input. The approach is limited to
English data.
5 Conclusion
We presented a robust parser. Future work will address multilingual input.
