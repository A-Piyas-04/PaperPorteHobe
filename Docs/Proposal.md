# Research Landscape Explorer

## Why This Project Matters

**Searching papers is easy.**  
**Understanding the landscape is hard.**

Students, early researchers, and decision-makers can find papers. But they still struggle to see the bigger picture, spot movement in a field, and decide where to explore next.

### 1. Scattered Information
Relevant papers are spread across many searches, dashboards, and sources.

### 2. No Clear Big Picture
Users can find papers, but cannot easily see the main areas and how they connect.

### 3. Slow Manual Exploration
Understanding trends and investigating promising directions takes too much time.

## Who feels this pain most?

| User Group | Need |
|---|---|
| Students | Need faster topic discovery |
| Early Researchers | Need quick field orientation |
| Research Teams | Need better theme tracking |
| Technical Recruiters | Need awareness of active research directions |

> **Core Motivation:** Turn paper discovery from keyword hunting into landscape understanding.

---

# What Already Exists — and What Is Still Missing

**The need is not for yet another search tool.**  
**It is for a clearer research exploration workflow.**

Useful tools already exist, but they usually solve only one part of the user journey.

## What users already have

### Paper Search
**Examples:** Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, arXiv  
Great for finding papers by keyword.

### Research Analytics & Open Data
**Examples:** OpenAlex Explore, Dimensions  
Useful for counts, metadata, and research trends.

### Topic Maps & Discovery Tools
**Examples:** Connected Papers, Litmaps, ResearchRabbit, Paperscape  
Helpful for visual exploration and related-paper discovery.

## What users still struggle with

### Fragmented Experience
Search, trends, and discovery often stay separate.

### Weak Context
Users see results, but not the bigger landscape around them.

### Decision Uncertainty
Even after searching, users still do not know where to explore next.

## Our Positioning

We are not replacing existing tools like Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, or Litmaps.  
**We are combining what users need into one clearer exploration experience.**

## Why this proposal is valuable

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Connected Workflow | From search to understanding |
| Evidence-Based Exploration | See the papers behind the signal |
| User-Centered Clarity | Built for real decision-making |

# What We Are Proposing

**A research exploration experience that helps users move from search to insight to action.**

Research Landscape Explorer helps users understand a field, explore promising directions, and make more confident decisions.

## What the user can do

### 1. Start with an interest
Enter a topic or question.

### 2. See the landscape
View the main research areas around it.

### 3. Understand movement
See which areas are active or changing.

### 4. Investigate deeper
Inspect papers behind interesting signals.

### 5. Decide next steps
Choose what to study, explore, or discuss.

## What value this creates

| Value | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Faster Exploration | Reduce time spent manually scanning literature. |
| Better Orientation | Build a clearer picture of a field. |
| More Confident Decisions | Support thesis, research, and hiring discussions. |
| Stronger Evidence | Back exploration with visible papers and context. |

> **Proposal Outcome:** A more useful way to explore research than searching paper-by-paper alone.
